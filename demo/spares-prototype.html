Calculation rules

  • Only Satisfied (including the spelling Satified) is fulfilled.
  • Part units are the sum of CustReqParts.Qty. Assembly quantities are not a multiplier.
  • Requests without part lines remain in request totals.
  • Product and PartID together identify a part. Repeated request lines are retained.
  • Names missing from manufacturer or factory lookups remain in the analysis.
